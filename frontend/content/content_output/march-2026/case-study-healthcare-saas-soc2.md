# Illustrative Scenario: Healthcare SaaS SOC 2 Readiness

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical healthcare software team receives a customer request for a SOC 2 report covering its hosted application. Its engineers already operate access and deployment controls, but ownership and evidence are scattered between tools.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Confirm the requested report

Ask the buyer which product and report period it needs. Review the proposed system boundary with the CPA firm before treating a sales deadline as an assessment schedule.

### Separate healthcare obligations

Map the information handled by the service and identify the contractual role with appropriate advisers. Keep HIPAA responsibilities visible alongside the SOC 2 work instead of describing one report as satisfying both.

### Sample operating records

Trace an access review and a production change from request through approval and completion. Find missing records before assuming that a written policy reflects consistent operation.

### Prepare the handoff

Create an evidence index for the agreed scope and assign owners for follow-up questions. Keep production changes within the existing approval process.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- The buyer, sponsor and auditor understand the same system boundary.
- Selected controls have reviewable records for the relevant period.
- Recurring reviews and unresolved exceptions have accountable owners.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

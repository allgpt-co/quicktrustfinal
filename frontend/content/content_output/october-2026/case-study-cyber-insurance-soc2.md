# Illustrative Scenario: Reusing SOC 2 Records for Insurance Review

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical software company has security-assessment evidence and is preparing an insurance application. It wants to reuse relevant records while respecting the different questions each review asks.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Compare the requirements

Map insurer questions to the specific controls and systems covered by existing records. Identify questions that the assessment evidence does not answer.

### Check freshness and scope

Review whether the evidence still reflects the current environment. Explain changes after the assessment period and obtain updated records where needed.

### Document gaps honestly

Record partial coverage and unresolved work for the authorized reviewer. Do not infer an insurance representation solely from the existence of a SOC 2 report.

### Keep commercial conclusions separate

Allow the insurer and business owner to evaluate the application and quotation. Track any actual change in terms independently from implementation activity.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- Reused records answer the insurer’s specific question.
- Material environment changes are reflected in the evidence.
- No premium reduction or coverage decision is attributed to the scenario.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

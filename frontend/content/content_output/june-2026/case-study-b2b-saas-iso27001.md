# Illustrative Scenario: SaaS ISO 27001 Implementation

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical B2B SaaS team is evaluating ISO 27001 because prospective customers ask about its information security management system. The company has technical safeguards but an inconsistent risk and review process.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Define the management-system boundary

Identify the service, supporting teams and supplier dependencies. Explain exclusions and obtain agreement from the business sponsor.

### Make risk decisions traceable

Use a consistent assessment method and assign owners to treatment actions. Document who can accept residual risk and when decisions require review.

### Connect controls to operations

Align policy commitments and applicability decisions with actual access, change and supplier processes. Sample evidence to find gaps between documents and behavior.

### Review effectiveness

Plan internal review, management decisions and corrective actions before independent assessment. Confirm the certification-body schedule and evidence expectations separately.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- The scope is understandable to operational owners.
- Risk treatment and control applicability decisions are linked.
- Findings have corrective actions and a documented effectiveness check.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

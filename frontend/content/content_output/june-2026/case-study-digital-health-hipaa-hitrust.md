# Illustrative Scenario: Healthcare HIPAA and HITRUST Planning

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical digital health provider receives a hospital request for HITRUST assurance while reviewing its own HIPAA responsibilities. The team wants to reuse evidence without confusing the two requirements.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Clarify the customer request

Ask which HITRUST assessment and product scope the hospital accepts. Confirm that choice with an authorized assessor before selecting an implementation sequence.

### Map healthcare information

Review data flows, suppliers and access paths with the relevant legal and compliance owners. Record the organization’s role and applicable agreements.

### Coordinate shared controls

Identify where an access review, incident exercise or recovery test can support both workstreams. Preserve separate applicability decisions and evidence expectations.

### Maintain the program

Assign ongoing owners for changes in data handling and suppliers. Keep assessment findings and operational exceptions in a reviewable backlog.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- HITRUST scope and healthcare obligations remain separately documented.
- Evidence reuse has a requirement-specific rationale.
- No certificate or report is represented as replacing ongoing legal responsibilities.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

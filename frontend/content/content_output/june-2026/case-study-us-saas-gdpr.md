# Illustrative Scenario: GDPR Data-Handling Readiness

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical US SaaS provider plans to serve European customers. Its team needs to understand data flows and contractual responsibilities before making privacy commitments in procurement responses.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Map the processing

Document information collected, purposes, recipients, retention and deletion paths. Include support access and subprocessors rather than only the main application database.

### Obtain legal interpretation

Ask qualified advisers to determine applicable roles, obligations, agreements and transfer arrangements. Keep those decisions linked to the actual service.

### Align technical behavior

Review access, retention and deletion implementation against the approved commitments. Define validation for records that propagate into backups or downstream services.

### Review customer responses

Update questionnaire answers and contract inputs to match the agreed position. Assign an owner to revisit them after material service changes.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- The data-flow map includes relevant service dependencies.
- Legal decisions are distinguished from engineering tasks.
- Published and contractual commitments match validated behavior.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

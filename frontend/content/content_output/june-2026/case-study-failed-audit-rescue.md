# Illustrative Scenario: Responding to SOC 2 Findings

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical service organization receives assessment findings that do not match its internal readiness dashboard. The team needs to understand the evidence gaps and agree a defensible remediation approach.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Read the finding precisely

Confirm the affected control, scope, period and evidence with the auditor. Distinguish an absent process from an incomplete record of a process.

### Preserve the original evidence

Retain the records reviewed and document the explanation of the gap. Do not backdate approvals or create retrospective operating records.

### Repair the operating process

Assign corrective actions with a specific owner and validation method. Update policy language only after the intended behavior is agreed and implemented.

### Agree the next review

Ask the auditor what evidence can support the corrective action and what implications the finding has for the examination. Do not promise a particular opinion or completion date.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- The finding and root cause are understood by the owner.
- Corrective actions have current, authentic evidence.
- Auditor decisions remain separate from implementation task closure.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

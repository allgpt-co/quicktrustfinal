# Illustrative Scenario: Cyber Insurance Control Evidence

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical SaaS company is preparing an insurance renewal questionnaire. Its team wants to answer control questions accurately and identify evidence gaps before making representations to the insurer.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Review the actual questionnaire

Identify the specific control assertions and who is authorized to approve them. Ask the broker or insurer to clarify ambiguous wording.

### Validate technical claims

Sample relevant access, endpoint, backup and incident-response records. Explain partial coverage rather than treating a tool purchase as complete implementation.

### Resolve discrepancies

Assign implementation tasks where statements and behavior do not match. Keep evidence of the validation and any approved clarification.

### Evaluate the quotation

Compare coverage, conditions, exclusions and pricing using the actual offer. Do not assume an assurance report automatically reduces premiums.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- Questionnaire responses are backed by scoped evidence.
- The authorized business owner approves representations.
- Any claimed financial benefit comes from an actual quotation rather than this scenario.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

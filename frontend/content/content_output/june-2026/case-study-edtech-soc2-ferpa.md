# Illustrative Scenario: EdTech Security and Privacy Scoping

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical education software team receives a school customer’s security and privacy questionnaire. The team needs to distinguish technical controls, contract commitments and legal questions about student information.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Map the information

Identify the data collected, intended users, access roles and service providers. Include support and analytics workflows that may receive information.

### Assign legal questions

Have qualified advisers determine applicable student-privacy, children’s-privacy and contractual obligations. Avoid assuming a SOC 2 report answers those questions.

### Implement the approved controls

Translate the agreed handling, access and retention commitments into owned technical work. Validate behavior with appropriate test data.

### Prepare accurate responses

Link questionnaire answers to current evidence and approved legal positions. Identify unknowns explicitly instead of selecting a favorable answer without support.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- Data handling is understood across application and support workflows.
- Legal and security review responsibilities are separated.
- Customer responses reflect the validated service and contract scope.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

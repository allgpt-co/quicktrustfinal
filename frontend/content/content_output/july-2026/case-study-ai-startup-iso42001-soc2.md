# Illustrative Scenario: AI Governance and SOC 2 Coordination

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical AI product team faces questions about model use, data handling and general security controls. It wants to coordinate an AI management system with a SOC 2 readiness program without assuming the scopes are identical.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Inventory AI uses

List customer-facing features, internal tools and third-party models. Record intended use, affected people, data handling and the person authorized to approve each use.

### Separate governance decisions

Identify AI-specific impact, oversight and evaluation questions in addition to access and software-delivery controls. Assign escalation paths for unexpected behavior.

### Reuse evidence carefully

Map shared release, supplier and incident records to the relevant requirement. Preserve the additional AI evaluation context and the SOC 2 system boundary.

### Plan independent reviews

Confirm the roles of the CPA firm and certification body. Prepare distinct evidence indexes where needed and avoid promising simultaneous outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- AI uses and accountable decision owners are identified.
- Shared evidence retains its purpose and scope.
- Release criteria and escalation decisions can be traced to records.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

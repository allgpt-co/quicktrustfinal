# Illustrative Scenario: Federal Cloud Readiness Scoping

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical cloud software provider is evaluating a federal customer opportunity. It needs to understand the applicable assurance path and service boundary before committing to a readiness project.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Confirm the opportunity requirements

Discuss the requested status, service use and procurement conditions with the customer and appropriate specialists. Consult current official program guidance for the applicable path.

### Define the cloud boundary

Inventory the service, administrative components, information flows and supporting providers. Document inherited and retained responsibilities.

### Assess the implementation work

Review control gaps and evidence needs with qualified advisers. Sequence dependencies such as identity boundaries, logging and operational procedures.

### Separate readiness from authorization

Record the decisions owned by external assessors, the customer and the program. Do not represent an implementation plan as a designation or authorization.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- The applicable path and required external decisions are identified.
- The service boundary and responsibility map are reviewable.
- Schedule and outcome assumptions remain conditional on verified requirements.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

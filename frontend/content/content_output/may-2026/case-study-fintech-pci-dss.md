# Illustrative Scenario: Fintech Payment-Scope Readiness

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical fintech team plans a new payment integration. Its payment partner asks for clarity about the application boundary and evidence of security controls before approving the arrangement.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Draw the transaction flow

Identify where account data enters, which services handle it, and which components can affect the payment flow. Include administrative access and supporting infrastructure.

### Confirm validation responsibilities

Discuss the applicable requirements and validation approach with the payment partner and qualified assessor. Do not infer scope solely from a hosted payment component.

### Assign technical work

Create scoped tasks for access, network boundaries, logging and change review where gaps are identified. Define verification and rollback before changing production.

### Package evidence

Connect each requested record to the relevant system and owner. Explain the responsibilities retained by the fintech team and those assigned to service providers.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- The payment boundary and responsibilities are documented.
- Technical changes have approvals and validation records.
- The responsible assessor can identify the evidence relevant to the selected validation path.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

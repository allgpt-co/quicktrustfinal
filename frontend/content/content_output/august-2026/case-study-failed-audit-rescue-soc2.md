# Illustrative Scenario: Testing a SOC 2 Readiness Dashboard

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical software team reports a high internal readiness score but cannot explain how several green controls were verified. Before relying on the score, its sponsor requests a control-by-control evidence review.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Select representative controls

Choose access, deployment, recovery and supplier examples. Trace each from policy through operation and ask someone outside the implementation team to review the record.

### Challenge status assumptions

Check whether a green indicator means a configuration was detected, a task was closed, or evidence was independently reviewed. Record those distinctions.

### Fix evidence and implementation gaps

Separate missing context from missing control behavior. Assign engineering work where needed rather than uploading an unrelated screenshot to clear the finding.

### Change the reporting model

Report reviewed controls, open work and accepted exceptions separately. Require a clear definition for any aggregate readiness measure.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- Every sampled status has an understandable evidence basis.
- Automated checks and human review decisions are distinguishable.
- The score is not represented as an audit opinion.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

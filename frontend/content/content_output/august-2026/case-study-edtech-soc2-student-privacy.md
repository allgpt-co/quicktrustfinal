# Illustrative Scenario: School Procurement Evidence Review

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical education vendor has documented its privacy scope and now needs to organize a school procurement review. The challenge is delivering evidence that is relevant, current and appropriate to share.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Group the buyer questions

Separate identity, data handling, service continuity and assurance-report requests. Assign reviewers who can approve each response.

### Check the source of each answer

Confirm the product, environment, period and owner of the supporting record. Remove outdated claims inherited from an earlier questionnaire.

### Control disclosure

Use redacted records or scoped descriptions where appropriate. Agree confidentiality arrangements before sharing restricted operational material.

### Maintain the response package

Record a review date and triggers for reapproval. Product and supplier changes should prompt a check of affected answers.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- Every material answer has a current source and approver.
- Restricted information follows an agreed sharing process.
- The package makes no unsupported procurement or revenue outcome claim.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist.

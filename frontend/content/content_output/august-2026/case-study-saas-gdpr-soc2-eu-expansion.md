# Illustrative Scenario: European Procurement and SOC 2 Evidence

> **Illustrative scenario — not a verified customer case study.** This hypothetical example describes proposed work. It reports no actual customer, testimonial, certification, revenue, savings, or delivery timeline.

## The planning situation

A hypothetical SaaS vendor receives a European customer questionnaire requesting both privacy information and security assurance. The vendor wants an organized review package without overstating what its SOC 2 evidence establishes.

## Proposed implementation approach

### Separate the requests

List privacy, contractual and security-assurance questions independently. Identify the person qualified to approve each answer.

### Check assurance scope

Confirm the relevant product and period of any available report. Explain exclusions and avoid presenting the report as proof of every privacy obligation.

### Prepare controlled disclosure

Choose the public materials, confidential documents and scoped explanations suitable for each reviewer. Agree restrictions before sharing sensitive records.

### Create a maintained answer library

Attach source evidence, owner and review date to reusable responses. Trigger reapproval when the product, supplier or processing arrangement changes.

## Acceptance criteria to verify

- Each response has an appropriate owner and source.
- Report scope and privacy obligations are not conflated.
- Confidential evidence has an agreed sharing process.

## Apply the example to your organization

Replace the assumptions with your own systems, requirements and owners. Confirm the relevant assessment or legal questions with the responsible specialist. An implementation task is complete only when the agreed behavior and evidence have been reviewed; this scenario does not imply an assessment result.

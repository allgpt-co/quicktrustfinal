## Plan a useful team discussion

Choose one requirement or control question. Ask participants to bring the current workflow, a sample evidence record, and any unresolved ownership decisions. End with a short action list rather than a general completion score.

For broader reading, browse the [guides](/resources/guides) and [illustrative scenarios](/resources/case-studies). These links are articles and planning resources, not webinar recordings.

## Enquire about a briefing

Use [Contact](/contact) to describe the topic, audience, and questions you would like to address. Availability, format, dates, and any commercial terms must be agreed separately. Do not send confidential customer material in the initial enquiry.

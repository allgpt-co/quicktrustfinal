## Examples for discussing implementation choices

The articles in this collection are **illustrative, hypothetical scenarios**. They are not verified customer case studies, testimonials, or records of completed engagements. They do not establish achieved certifications, revenue, savings, or delivery timelines.

Use a scenario to examine a problem similar to yours and decide what information an implementation team would need. The useful output is a set of scope questions, control decisions, evidence needs, and acceptance criteria.

## Choose the closest operating problem

Healthcare scenarios explore data handling, assurance requests, and ownership across clinical or software workflows. Fintech scenarios examine payment boundaries and shared control evidence. SaaS scenarios cover enterprise security reviews, international expansion, and remediation. Other examples focus on governance, educational software, or insurance-related evidence.

Each scenario separates the hypothetical situation from the proposed work and the criteria that would need to be verified. A suggested implementation sequence is not a promised schedule.

## Use scenarios in a working session

1. Identify which assumptions match your organization and which do not.
2. Replace the example scope with your actual systems, data flows, and obligations.
3. Assign an owner to every unresolved question.
4. Define the record that would demonstrate each proposed control.
5. Review the plan with the relevant business owner and assessor before making commitments.

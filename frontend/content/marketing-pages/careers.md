## Work that connects security and delivery

QuickTrust's work spans compliance planning, security engineering, DevOps, and evidence operations. The common thread is turning a requirement into a practical control that a team can operate and a reviewer can understand.

No specific vacancies are listed on this page. An expression of interest does not indicate that a role is open or guarantee a response or interview.

## What to include in an enquiry

Describe the kind of work you are interested in, your relevant experience, and a public portfolio or professional profile if you wish to share one. Examples of useful experience include implementing access reviews, improving a deployment process, documenting a recovery exercise, or coordinating evidence across teams.

Use the [contact form](/contact) for an initial careers enquiry. Do not include identity documents, banking information, confidential customer records, or credentials. Role details, location, employment terms, and the selection process would be discussed for a specific opportunity.

Read [how the implementation team works](/company/team) and [what QuickTrust does](/about) to understand the delivery model before contacting us.

## From a requirement to an operating control

Use [policy gap analysis](/solutions/policy-gap-analysis) to compare commitments with actual practice. Then define the engineering or process work needed to close each gap. [Evidence collection](/solutions/evidence-collection-automation) helps make that work reviewable, and [continuous monitoring](/solutions/continuous-compliance-monitoring) keeps ownership visible after the initial assessment.

A productive reading session ends with a decision: the scope to confirm, the owner to involve, the evidence to find, or the change to implement. Avoid treating a long checklist as proof that a control is operating.

## Buying and implementation decisions

If you are selecting a platform, compare responsibilities as well as features. The [Secureframe](/compare/quicktrust-vs-secureframe), [Sprinto](/compare/quicktrust-vs-sprinto), and [Thoropass](/compare/quicktrust-vs-thoropass) guides provide questions for a requirements-based evaluation.

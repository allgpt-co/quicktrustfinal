## Coordinate around a clear customer scope

Compliance projects often involve a customer's internal team, implementation specialists, technology providers, and an independent assessor. A useful working relationship starts with clear responsibilities and permission to share the relevant information.

This page invites partnership enquiries. It does not publish a directory of confirmed partners or imply endorsement, accreditation, or an existing commercial relationship with any named provider.

## Define the handoff

For an implementation and assessment engagement, agree who defines scope, who makes operational changes, who reviews evidence, and who issues the assessment result. Preserve the independence and eligibility requirements of the assessor. Record escalation and confidentiality arrangements before exchanging customer material.

For technology-related work, describe the system, integration boundary, support responsibilities, and the evidence needed to validate the workflow. A technical integration is not, by itself, a partnership agreement.

## Make an enquiry

Use [Contact](/contact) to describe your organization, the proposed collaboration, and the customer problem it would address. Share an outline rather than confidential customer records. Commercial terms, referral arrangements, and joint claims require a separate agreement.
